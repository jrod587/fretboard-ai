# Tonecrafted - Feature Backlog

## Low Hanging Fruit Ideas

1. **Interactive Circle of Fifths**
   - A clickable circle that shows relative minors, major chords in a key, and highlights the fretboard notes for that key.
   - Turns a static chart into an interactive discovery tool.

2. **Strumming Pattern Library**
   - A visual library of 10-20 essential strumming patterns (The "Campfire Strum", the "Folk Strum", the "Reggae Skank").
   - Features: Down/Up arrows, audio clip, list of songs using that pattern.

3. **Common Chord Progression Generator (Roman Numeral Decoder)**
   - Select a Key and a common progression (e.g., I-IV-V or ii-V-I).
   - Instantly shows the exact chords to play and highlights them on the fretboard.
   - Teaches song structure without heavy theory.

4. **"Tune By Ear" Reference Room**
   - Simple page playing perfect pitches for standard tuning (E A D G B E) and common alternate tunings (Drop D, Open G, DADGAD).
   - Encourages ear training over relying entirely on visual tuners.
