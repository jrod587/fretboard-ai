The March (4/4 Time)

This is the bedrock of modern music. It feels like a steady, relentless march. When musicians say a song is in '4/4', they just mean there's a heavy pulse every 4 beats. It's the groove you already know by heart.

ICONIC EXAMPLES
  • Smells Like Teen Spirit (Nirvana)
  • Knockin' on Heaven's Door (Bob Dylan)
  • Almost everything else on the radio
PRACTICE: Try strumming a simple G chord on the '1' of every cycle.
